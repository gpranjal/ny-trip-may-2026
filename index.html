The Route

The Itinerary

Five days, told day by day.

Each day with its drive, its anchor stops, the night's lodging — and the small notes that come from travelling with a 2-year-old.

01
Mon May 18 Boston — Ithaca ~6 hr · 309 mi

A long drive west, rewarded by Taughannock’s taller-than-Niagara plunge.

An eight o’clock departure from Marlborough, lunch in Albany, and an arrival in Ithaca by four. With Vasu napping through the worst of it, plan on roughly seven hours door to door. Then the easy reward: a stroller-friendly mile and a half along the gorge floor to the base of Taughannock Falls, 215 feet of straight cascade. Dinner on the Commons.

Verified Drive ~6 hr · 309 mi · before stops
Lodging Ithaca
02
Tue May 19 Ithaca — Geneseo ~2½ hr

Two showcase parks — Watkins Glen’s gorge, then Letchworth’s seventeen-mile rim.

Morning at Watkins Glen: enter from the North Rim (bridge construction in 2026), Vasu in the carrier on the stone steps, Gauri taking them by hand. Afternoon at Letchworth, the “Grand Canyon of the East”: drive the seventeen-mile park road, stop at Inspiration Point and Middle Falls. Both are stroller-easy. Stay near the Mt. Morris gate.

Verified Drive ~2½ hr · Ithaca–WG ~40 min
Lodging Geneseo / Mt Morris
03
Wed May 20 Geneseo — Alex Bay ~3¾ hr · 184 mi

North to the islands, and a boat tour to a hundred-and-twenty-room castle.

A steady three-hour drive north, with a Syracuse lunch break, brings you to Alexandria Bay by early afternoon. From the dock, Uncle Sam Boat Tours runs a shuttle to Boldt Castle on Heart Island — the single most memorable Thousand Islands experience. Castle admission verified at $14 adult (free under five). Family of six total: about $112.50 with shuttle. End the day at Wellesley Island for sunset over the St. Lawrence.

Verified Drive ~3 hr 47 min · 184 mi
Lodging Alexandria Bay
04
Thu May 21 Alex Bay — Lake Placid ~3½ hr

River to mountains. High Falls Gorge, the Olympic Skyride, and Mirror Lake at dusk.

A morning out of Alexandria Bay, into the Adirondacks. Stop at High Falls Gorge in Wilmington (2026 season opens May 3 — just in time). Bridges over the AuSable for Gauri; carrier for Vasu on the steeper bits. In Lake Placid, the Cloudsplitter Gondola is closed until June 26, so the Olympic Jumping Complex Skyride is the substitute. Evening: a slow loop around Mirror Lake, paved and fully accessible.

Verified Drive ~3½ hr · with High Falls stop
Lodging Lake Placid
05
Fri May 22 Lake Placid — Marlborough ~5½ hr drive

A long drive home, by way of Saratoga’s bubbling springs.

Leave Lake Placid by eight if you want any cushion at all. Five and a half hours of driving plus lunch, a walk, and rest stops adds up to seven or eight hours door to door — tight with kids. Late morning at Saratoga Spa State Park (Geyser Creek, the carbonated mineral “spouters”); lunch at Hattie’s. Cross the Hudson before three to dodge afternoon Albany traffic.

Open question: the Veterans Memorial Highway up Whiteface may open today (Memorial Day weekend Friday). Worth a phone call to ORDA. Squeezing it in adds 90 minutes to an already-tight day.
Door to Door ~7–8 hr · leave by 8 AM
